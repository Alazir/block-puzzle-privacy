Block Puzzle - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains how the Block Puzzle game for Android ("the app"), developed by Gustavo Marques ("we"), handles information.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information such as your name, email or location. The app has no account or login. Your scores, records, game progress and settings are saved only on your device and are never sent to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases such as "Remove ads" are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play's own privacy policy applies to these payments.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and deletion

We do not sell or share your data. Data processed by Google for ads and purchases is handled under Google's policies. Uninstalling the app deletes all game data stored on your device.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
